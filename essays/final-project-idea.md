## Problem + Solution

**Main problem:** Students and faculty often email the school about problems, but students do not know whether the school is aware of them. The school does not really have an open forum for the people on campus. 

**Solution:** Create a forum where anyone with a UH email can submit a ticket. Other users can comment on or upvote shared issues, and the school can flag issues as known or solved. Requires a UH email to make a ticket, comment or like another person's ticket. For the school, they can flag a ticket as seen or solved. Basically, a person can make a ticket, if people have the same problem they can like the ticket and it will push the ticket up in the forums which will help show the school what problems they prioritize and address.
