The whole premise of this project was for my groupmates and me to capture a scene that tells a story within a picture. My group consisted of myself, a Senior, two Juniors, and one Freshman. After a few days and weeks of planning and thinking my group decided on our scene. 

The idea, or rather, story of the scene was that a chair that had turned into a monster would attempt to eat me. My groupmates would then try to get me out of the monster chair by holding its mouth open with weapons and attempting to pull me out of the monster's mouth. 

Another theme in our story was that the monster chair represented life after high school. So me getting eaten by the chair represented the things that come after high school, such as work, college, and more. This ties into me being the senior student of the project, and the underclassmen attempting to get me out of the monster chair's mouth represents them trying to bring me back to high school. 

The main photography practices our photography teacher, Ms. Ednah Folk, wanted us to do were cropping, Photoshop, and scene setting.
